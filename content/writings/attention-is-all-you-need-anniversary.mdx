“Attention Is All You Need”

On this day 8 years ago — June 12, 2017 — the paper “Attention Is All You Need” was published on arXiv and it marks a turning point in human history.

It introduced the Transformer architecture — no recurrence, no convolutions, just self-attention — and unknowingly ignited the foundation for nearly every generative AI model we use today.

From OpenAI's ChatGPT to Claude, Gemini, LLaMA, Mistral, and beyond — they all trace their DNA back to this one idea.

Some fun trivia that makes it even more legendary:

- The paper was written by a team of engineers and researchers at Google Brain and Google Translate.
- It was initially intended for machine translation — and only later did people realize its potential across everything.
- The original paper had just 6 attention heads and 65 million parameters — today’s models have billions of parameters and attention heads in the hundreds.
- It was released with little fanfare… until people started running benchmarks — and the results blew everything else away.

Now, 8 years later, we’re building agents, copilots, reasoning engines, and multi-modal AIs — all rooted in that architecture.
