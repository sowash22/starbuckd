This is from Silicon Valley — one of my favorite shows and always ahead of its time.

An AI agent deletes the entire codebase to “fix” bugs, which is statistically correct: zero code means zero bugs.

Then it orders 4,000 pounds of meat while trying to find cheap hamburgers.

It’s hilarious, but still painfully relevant. AI agents, much like Son of Anton, will confidently do exactly what you ask — especially if it’s not what you meant. Without clear goals, guardrails, and evaluation, AI won’t just miss the point — it’ll scale the failure.

As someone building agents and infrastructure, this scene hits close to home. The challenge is simply multi-fold compared to traditional SaaS. Delivering scalable, reliable, and safe systems with agentic capabilities is harder than people think — and we’ve barely scratched the surface.
